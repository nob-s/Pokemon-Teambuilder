# Pokemon Teambuilder 
Link: https://nob-s.github.io/Pokemon-Teambuilder/

Pokemon Teambuilder helps evaluate your pokemon team and gives suggestions to improve it!

The window in the bottom left shows your team summary:
- Your team's high and low average stats
- Your team's weakness to a single type
- Your pokemon with extremely high or extremely low stats

## Overview

### Approach and architecture
Similar to MVC concept, modelling Pokemon, PokemonParser to parse API data, and UI that takes
from the model.

### Setup Instructions
No setup! Just visit the website!

### Testing Instructions
Build you own Pokemon team to test.

### Assumptions
I assumed some stats are not changed and fixed, unlike competitive Pokemon, like level, EVs, IVs
Assumed no natures, and in general a simple model since Pokemon is quite complex

### Challenges
Since it is pretty much my first react ts vite project, (I had 1-digit hours before this), learning was tough but fun.
Specifically trying to implement cookies was difficult since managing the order to load data was difficult.
